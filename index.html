ZIFTO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. About ZIFTO

ZIFTO is a geometric puzzle game for Android. Players cut, rotate, and arrange shapes to solve puzzles.

2. Information and Storage

ZIFTO does not require account registration. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the user's device using application storage.

3. Data Sharing

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics services, or an online account system. ZIFTO does not intentionally transmit game progress or personal information to the developer's servers.

4. Internet Connection

The game's core puzzle functionality is designed to work offline.

5. Children's Privacy

ZIFTO does not knowingly request personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored on the device. Users can clear the app's storage or uninstall ZIFTO to remove locally stored game data, subject to any device backup or restoration settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the ZIFTO Google Play listing.
